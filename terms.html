Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026-06-02

What This Site Is / Is Not

This is a global anonymous public sentiment reference tool that shows "support / oppose" click trends from site visitors. It is not a scientific sample poll, an official election, or a product of a polling organization. Results do not represent any official assessment, election forecast, or true public opinion share.

Nature of Results

Because of the anonymous design, this site cannot strictly guarantee "one person, one vote." Rankings use minimum vote thresholds and confidence correction but may still be affected by vote manipulation, bots, or uneven geographic distribution. Treat results as reference indicators, not precise measurements.

Prohibited Conduct

  • Automated vote manipulation, bulk scripts, or abuse of proxies/datacenter IPs to skew results;
  • Paid votes or payment to raise or hide a leader's approval rate;
  • Impersonating this site or official bodies, or misrepresenting our results as formal polls;
  • Using site data for formal political campaigning or commercial use without attribution.
