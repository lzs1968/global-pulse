Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-02 · We follow a "collect as little data as possible" principle.

What We Do Not Collect

This site requires no registration. We do not collect real names, government ID numbers, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location, and we do not build identifiable political profiles of any individual.

Limited Data Processed to Provide the Service

To limit vote manipulation and keep the service secure, we process:

  • Network identifiers used for visitor deduplication (e.g. IP-derived signals), used only for "one vote per leader, changeable" limits and not linked to real identity;
  • Browser local storage (localStorage) to remember your choices and language preferences on this device;
  • Aggregated visit and abnormal traffic metrics for anti-fraud and capacity planning.

Cookies & Tracking

This site does not use cross-site behavioral advertising cookies or political-targeted ads. If we introduce contextual ads or analytics in the future, we will explain them here and provide choices.

Data Retention

Vote deduplication records are kept only as long as needed for vote limits; fraud-related abnormal events are retained briefly then deleted or anonymized. Aggregated statistics may be kept long-term but contain no individual-level identifiers.

Aggregated Display, No Sale of Personal Data

Public pages show only aggregated country/region or leader-level results. We do not sell individual-level vote records or infer and sell anyone's political views based on their vote.

Your Rights

Under applicable law in your region (e.g. GDPR/CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing. Because this site is designed to be anonymous and usually cannot link data to a specific person, you may submit requests via the contact form; we will handle them within reason.

Minors

This site is intended for the general public, is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
